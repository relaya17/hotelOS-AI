## Short explanation

HotelOS AI is sold as an **intelligence layer above the PMS** — not a replacement for Opera / Protel / Fidelio / Clock.

- One **USD** list for every country (including Israel) — no separate local-currency price list.
- **Pilot** = short proof of value on one hotel.
- **Network** = ongoing subscription after proof.
- **Enterprise** = groups that need trust/legal pack + multiple properties.

Pricing sits **below a full PMS** and inside a normal band for ops overlays worldwide.
